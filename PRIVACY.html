Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applicable law: Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data (Dahir 1-09-15 of 18 February 2009) and the supervision of the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel).

1. Who is responsible for this website?

Soufyane El Haddaoui (SEH World), Morocco. Contact for any privacy question: souf2002yane@gmail.com or via our Contact Us page.

2. The short version

  • SEH World now uses Google Firebase to manage accounts, points and comments. When you create an account, your data is stored in Firebase (Firestore), not only in your browser.
  • If you play as Guest, you have 0 Points and nothing is saved on our server. Guest data stays only in your browser (localStorage).
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show personalized or non-personalized ads. You can manage your ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Optional anonymous statistics (Plausible) are used only if you click “Accept” on the banner.

4. Legal basis

Your consent (Law 09-08, article 4): you choose to create an account, you choose to accept or refuse optional statistics and personalized ads. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account on the INFO page, clearing your browser data, or using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

5. How long data is kept

Firebase account data (name, email, points) stays until you delete your account. Profile and Guest data stay on your device until you delete them (button “Delete My Account” on the INFO page, or clearing your browser data). Plausible does not keep individual visitor profiles.

When you delete your account, we delete your document from Firestore users / your-uid and your comments, within 30 days.

6. Cookies and advertising - Google AdSense

  • Strictly necessary browser storage (localStorage): profile, points, theme and your cookie choice. No consent is needed for these because you ask for the feature, and they stay on your device.
  • Firebase Authentication session: to keep you logged in securely. This is strictly necessary if you create an account.
  • Google AdSense: Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Plausible Analytics: cookie-free, loaded only after you accept.
  • We do not use social-media tracking pixels.

7. Other services you may contact

  • Google AdSense / Google LLC - shows ads on this site. Google processes data according to its Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore) - manages your account and points. Google processes data according to its Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Servers may be located outside Morocco (EU / USA).
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host it receives technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads a page.
  • Third-party images: some game pictures on the GAME page are loaded from other websites, which can see your IP address when the picture loads.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) delivers a script used by the “download story as image” button.
  • WhatsApp / Facebook are opened only if you click a share button.
  • When a security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) is enabled on a form, Cloudflare processes technical data to tell humans from bots.

Some of these providers are located outside Morocco (for example in the European Union or the United States). We only use them for the purposes above.

8. Security — please read

Your Firebase account is protected by Firebase Security Rules. Only you can change your own points. However, no system on the internet is 100% secure.

Your localStorage data is stored in your browser, so anyone who can use your device and browser may be able to see it. Do not use a password here that you use anywhere else (email, bank, social networks), and do not enter sensitive information. Use a private device or log out/delete your account on shared computers.

9. Children

SEH World is a general-audience entertainment website. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or filling in the profile form.

10. Your rights (Law 09-08, articles 7 to 9)

You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the use of your personal data. Most of this you can do yourself on the INFO page (“Edit My Info”, “Delete My Account”). For anything else, write to souf2002yane@gmail.com. You also have the right to file a complaint with the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, we will update this page and its date. See also our Terms of Use (CGU) and Contact Us.
